# Brag Plan: Money OS

## What is this app?
Money OS is a personal finance operating system — expense tracking, AI-powered natural-language expense entry, Apple Shortcuts/Siri integration, and bill splitting — wrapped in a dark "calm futurism" UI that also renders your spending as a sci-fi mission-control HUD, a 3D orbiting universe, and a mock police dossier on your worst habit.

## The angle
A rapid-fire tour, not a single joke. Money OS keeps escalating: type two words and AI logs the expense, your month becomes a spy-movie targeting HUD, your spending becomes a solar system, your worst habit gets a police file, and you can do all of it hands-free by talking to Siri. Every beat is real product copy and real UI — the humor is in the pace and the range, not one gimmick stretched thin.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
Hard cut to black. Crime-tape yellow flash. Text SLAMS in, oversized, all caps:
"PERSONAL FINANCE / WAS TOO CALM." (two lines — mirrors the chaotic-tone hook pattern, and reframes the video as "we made everything weird," not "here's one joke.")

## Key moments (the middle — six distinct features, back to back)
- **Reveal**: the Home dashboard's net balance counting up against the ink-black "calm futurism" surface.
- **AI auto-add**: typing "20 aamras" into the Money OS Assistant (⌘K) and watching it land as a parsed, categorized, saved expense card — no forms.
- **Pulse**: the spending-as-mission-control HUD screen — boot text typing "READING PURCHASE HISTORY," corner instrument brackets, "DRAG TO ORBIT · SCROLL TO ZOOM."
- **Money Universe**: spending categories as real 3D orbiting bodies, sized by actual spend.
- **Crime Scene**: "PRIMARY SUSPECT" stamped, then the real verdict line typing out — "Table for one. Bill for five."
- **Apple Shortcuts**: "Hey Siri, Log Expense" → speaking/typing "50 chai" → Money OS logs it and replies, hands-free.
- **Splits**: a "you owe" figure snapping to settled.

## Outro / punchline
"MONEY OS" wordmark slams full-screen on the ink-black background with a violet/blue glow. Tagline: "YOUR MONEY OPERATING SYSTEM. IT DOES A LOT." Hard cut to black.

## User flow worth showing
This video is structured as a feature tour rather than one linear flow: dashboard → AI-add → Pulse → Universe → Crime Scene → Shortcuts → Splits. Each is a real, working surface of the product, shown doing its actual job (typing an expense, dragging into a HUD, watching bodies orbit, reading a verdict, talking to Siri, watching a balance settle) — never a static marketing recreation.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: a feature-dense highlight reel played at full speed — "this app does WAY too much, and yes, all of it is real"
- Interpretation: fast cuts (≤3s per scene), hard/flash/zoom transitions, all-caps oversized type, short aggressive phrases, dense rhythmic music from frame 1. Nine scenes instead of the usual 6-8 because the point of this cut is breadth — pace stays snappy so it never drags.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: ~22.5s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0b0b10` (ink, dark mode default); Pulse scene uses a near-black `#04050a` HUD variant
- Accent: `#0A84FF` primary blue / `#7C5CFF` violet secondary; mint `#30D6A5` and amber `#FF9F0A` as supporting accents
- Text: `#f5f6fa` (dark mode) on ink; crime-tape yellow `#F5C518` and crime red `#FF3B30` for the Crime Scene beat; warm off-white `rgba(240,232,210,0.92)` for its body copy
- Display font: "Geist Variable" in the real app (self-hosted) — this composition substitutes "Archivo Black" (bundled, embeds deterministically) for all display/statement text
- Body/data font: "JetBrains Mono" (bundled) for money figures, HUD labels, assistant UI, and typed copy
- Strongest visual elements: the Money Universe 3D orbiting-bodies scene, the Pulse HUD, and the Crime Scene evidence-dossier styling

## Share copy (draft)
Money OS logs an expense from two typed words, turns your month into a 3D solar system, and opens a case file on your worst habit — and yes, Siri can do all of it hands-free. 🕵️💸

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, driving the whole edit
- Music: bundled track `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120.19 BPM), full energy from frame 1
- Music treatment: no fade-in; a brief dip under the Crime Scene verdict typing (≈12.7–14.5s) so that line stays legible; hard stop/fade on the final black frame
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Two strong-cue locks: the Splits settle-snap (~18.5s strong beat) and the outro wordmark slam (~20.0s strong beat). Money Universe's 4 orbiting bodies snap to consecutive beats in their scene window.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — Money Universe's orbital glow may breathe with RMS; nothing else reacts to audio
- SFX posture: dense, motion-matched — hard hits on every transition, keypress ticks under every typed line (AI-add, Crime Scene verdict, Shortcuts input), a success chime when the AI-add card lands and when the Shortcuts reply lands, a settle/snap sound for Splits
- Restraint rule: no competing music swell under the Crime Scene verdict line or the AI-add/Shortcuts reply text — those need to be read

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.0s
Full black frame. Crime-tape yellow flash bar sweeps once. Oversized all-caps text slams in: "PERSONAL FINANCE" / "WAS TOO CALM."
Audio-coupled idea: hard impact synced to the text slam.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal: the app — 2.0s
Home dashboard on the ink-black surface. Hero net-balance number counts up. "MONEY OS" wordmark stamps in.
Sequential/interaction: balance counts 0 → real figure in under a second.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — AI auto-add — 3.0s
The Money OS Assistant panel: input row typed live — "20 aamras" — character by character, then a parsed, categorized expense card slides in with a "Saved" badge. Caption: "TYPE IT. AI ADDS IT."
Sequential/interaction: typing → parsed card arrival, in that order.
Audio-coupled idea: keypress ticks while typing; a soft success chime when the card lands.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Pulse (sci-fi HUD) — 2.8s
Near-black mission-control screen: corner instrument brackets, boot text typing "READING PURCHASE HISTORY," a scattered glowing field of spending clusters, bottom prompt "DRAG TO ORBIT · SCROLL TO ZOOM." Caption/label: "PULSE."
Sequential/interaction: boot text types on; clusters fade up.
Transition mood: zoom cut → Scene 5 (biggest visual swing, matches the jump into a fully 3D scene)

### Scene 5 — Money Universe — 2.2s
Spending categories as 4 glowing orbiting bodies, sized by real amount, against deep ink space. Caption: "EVERY DOLLAR GETS A PLANET."
Sequential/interaction: bodies arrive one by one, beat-grid timed.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Crime Scene (verdict) — 2.8s
"PRIMARY SUSPECT" stamps in crime-tape yellow, suspect category slams in red, then the real verdict line types out: "Table for one. Bill for five."
Audio-coupled idea: stamp impact, then quiet keypress ticks under the typed line with no competing music swell.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Apple Shortcuts / Siri — 2.8s
A Shortcuts-style icon, "Hey Siri, Log Expense," then the real Shortcut's own prompt flow: typed/spoken "50 chai," then a logged confirmation reply. Caption: "SAY IT. SIRI LOGS IT."
Sequential/interaction: icon → prompt → typed input → reply, in that order.
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 8

### Scene 8 — Splits settle — 1.8s
A "YOU OWE $—" figure snaps to "SETTLED $0.00."
Audio-coupled idea: snap SFX beat-locked to a strong cue (~18.5s).
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 9

### Scene 9 — Outro / punchline — 3.2s
"MONEY OS" wordmark slams full-screen (beat-locked to a strong cue, ~20.0s) with a violet/blue glow. Tagline: "YOUR MONEY OPERATING SYSTEM. IT DOES A LOT." Fade to black at the very end (final-scene exception).

**Music mood for this video:** chaotic / upbeat "business moves" energy, full volume throughout except the Crime Scene verdict dip
**Audio summary:** Dense rhythmic bed at 120 BPM drives every hard cut; SFX are hits/whooshes on transitions and keypress ticks on every typed line, with two beat-locked payoffs (Splits settle, outro wordmark) and one deliberate quiet pocket under the Crime Scene verdict.
